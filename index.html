    Oxygen on the scale

    Oxygen only, in percent. Pressure differs by habitat. Compared with Earth air, 30% is more than 40% higher, which makes materials much more flammable.

    Not in the data yet

    • FM2, Flammability of Materials on the Moon: the first combustion experiment on another planetary body, at one-sixth gravity.
    • LUCI, Lunar-g Combustion Investigation: expected to add partial-gravity results.

    No partial-gravity combustion data is in PSI today. Microgravity and normal-gravity results bound what happens at Moon gravity. The one proxy is ACME E-FIELD Flames, which mimics partial gravity with an electric field.

    Rocket engines and propellants are outside this data: their combustion does not depend on gravity.

    What it means on Earth

    • Fine-water-mist extinguishers developed for the ISS are now used on Earth.
    • Fire detector: a 1970s shuttle smoke detector that tells smoke from dust, so it raises fewer nuisance alarms. It is used in mining.
    • Firefighter air tanks: 1970s spacesuit technology adapted for breathing gear.
    • Firefighter clothing: 1980s spacesuit technology applied to protective attire.
